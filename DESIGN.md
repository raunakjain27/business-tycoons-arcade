## 2. Art Direction System

### Palette: High-Voltage Cyber Matrix
- **`cyber-void`**: `#05070f` (Infinite depth space canvas)
- **`cyber-surface`**: `#0f1526` (Cockpit control console)
- **`cyber-card`**: `rgba(15, 22, 38, 0.85)` (Frosted laser-cut glass, 24px blur)
- **`neon-cyan`**: `#00f0ff` (Primary electric pulse, buttons, room keys)
- **`neon-magenta`**: `#ff0077` (Hot laser action, primary arcade pushbuttons)
- **`neon-green`**: `#00ff88` (Live network beacon, match launch, active ready pods)
- **`neon-amber`**: `#ffb703` (Bullion vault gold, high-roller badges, jackpot streaks)
- **`neon-purple`**: `#9d4edd` (Deep plasma atmosphere, board core aura)
- **`border-cyber`**: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)` (Laser hairline borders)

### Typography
- **Marquee & Headlines**: `Orbitron` (Weights 700 to 900, uppercase, text-shadow neon glow).
- **Subheads & Tactical Buttons**: `Space Grotesk` (Weights 600 to 800, tracked +0.8px).
- **Telemetry & Numbers**: `JetBrains Mono` (Tabular numerals for dice values, passphrases, countdowns).
- **Body & Captions**: `Outfit` (High legibility, clean readability).

## 3. High-Energy Awwwards Principles
1. **Dynamic Atmosphere at Rest**: The background is never flat or dead—perspective cyber gridlines, ambient plasma nebula, and CRT scanlines keep pixels alive at 60 fps.
2. **Real 3D Tactile Arcade Buttons**: Real arcade cabinet pushbuttons with 4px physical bevel bottom borders (`border-bottom: 4px solid ...`) that physically sink down on click with audio synthesis feedback.
3. **Live Telemetry & Activity Feed**: Streaming marquee streamer with active player counts, jackpot pools, and recent match winners.
4. **Holographic Fast-Access Keypads**: Monospace cyber room keys with 1-click clipboard integration and laser unlock buttons.
5. **Arcade Cabinet Showcase Cards**: Each of the 10 games features an illuminated top marquee, energy tags (`HIGH OCTANE`, `HYPER SPEED`, `MEGA JACKPOT`), and tactile `INSERT COIN & PLAY ▶` triggers.
6. **Zero Clutter, Maximum Impact**: Every element has an unambiguous hierarchy—clear status, immediate call-to-action, high contrast, zero cognitive friction.
